Banked but Underserved: Revolut's Strategic Case for South Africa

Strategy report for FINE 477, Fintech for Business and Finance

84% of South African adults have a bank account, yet most remain poorly served by the five banks that dominate the market. This report argues that Revolut should win there by being different, not just cheaper. It recommends using the PayShap instant-payment system to undercut the big banks' foreign exchange margins and build trust before its banking licence arrives, and launching a stablecoin remittance corridor for the UK–South Africa diaspora.

Are Viral Products Actually Worth It?

Group project for INSY 442, Data Analysis and Visualization

Do TikTok-viral beauty brands at Sephora actually satisfy customers, or just sell hype? Viral brands charged more and earned up to 35% more revenue per product, but weren't rated higher, and in skincare were rated lower. I came up with the project idea and led the data preparation in Excel: cleaning five datasets, filtering reviews to 2020 onward, fixing encoding errors, and building the virality flags behind our Power BI and Tableau dashboards.
